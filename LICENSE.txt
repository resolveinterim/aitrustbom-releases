5. Trial and Subscription

The Trial Period is free, requires no payment method, and starts when the
license service issues the trial key; it is available once per GitHub owner.
Paid Operations are approved `export` (except `export --draft`), `publish`
(including draft and `--from` modes), `pack` (including `--from`),
`answer` (all modes), and `drift`, including Action or workflow steps that
run those operations. They require a valid trial or paid entitlement. The
CLI checks entitlement before a Paid Operation does work and refuses it
without a valid entitlement, using exit code 4 and a `license-*` diagnostic.
A paid entitlement runs through the paid-through date. A grace period of up
to seven days applies only when an automatic subscription renewal has been
initiated and the payment provider is still processing that renewal at the
paid-through date. It does not apply to a canceled or non-renewing
Subscription or to an expired Trial Period. Cancellation stops future
renewal but does not shorten the already-paid billing period.

Free Operations do not require a trial or Subscription and remain available
after a trial or paid entitlement ends. They include `init`, `scan`, `gate`,
`readiness`, `request-approval`, `export --draft`,
the Docker-free Action's scan/gate, PR comment and check, `license`
management commands, and `version`. Free Operations do not check
entitlement, prompt for a key, or emit `license-*` diagnostics. The explicit
`license activate`, `license refresh`, and `license request-trial`
management commands may contact the license service; management failures
use exit code 2 without `license-*` diagnostics. Plain `license` and
`license status` remain offline.

You must not disable, tamper with, or circumvent entitlement checks, or

refresh, or Paid Operations. The Software sends only licensing data described
in the Privacy Policy for those requests; it does not send repository content.

The license is bound to one GitHub owner. Paid commands resolve that owner
from the repository's `origin` remote; GitHub Actions use
`GITHUB_REPOSITORY_OWNER`. Resolve Interim handles personal data involved
in license requests and verification as described in its Privacy Policy at
https://aitrustbom.com/legal/privacy/.

Output You have already generated and approved remains Yours under section 4.
